Our Story

From a borrowed office to Harbour Hall

Lagos Tech Mixer started in 2024 as 40 developers in a borrowed office. Two years later it's one of the friendliest tech days in Lagos: a full day of talks, demos and real connections for anyone who builds on the internet. No suits, no sales pitches, just people learning from each other.

  1. 2024

    First meetup: 40 developers in a borrowed office in Yaba

  2. 2025

    Moved to a hall: 300 attendees, 8 speakers, first sponsors

  3. 2026

    Harbour Hall, 600 seats, our biggest year yet

This Year's Keynote

Funmi Adeyemi

AI Engineer and founder

Funmi has spent eight years building software for banks, startups and global teams. She now runs a studio that helps African companies put AI to work in their products.

Her talk, "Build with AI, not around it", shows how developers can use AI tools every day without losing their craft.
